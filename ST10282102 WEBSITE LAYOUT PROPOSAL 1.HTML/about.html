Our History

Queens Cookies Bakery was founded in 2013 by Mamma Queen in Soweto, on Vilakazi Street.

What started as a small kitchen making homemade cookies quickly grew into a local bakery offering cookies, cakes, pastries, and delicious desserts.

Mamma Queen built her bakery around her love for baking and her desire to bring happiness to people through freshly baked treats.

Today, Queens Cookies Bakery aims to continue sharing delicious baked goods, creating memorable experiences, and building lasting relationships with customers and the local community.

Our Mission

To create fresh, delicious and high-quality baked goods that bring warmth and happiness to every customer.

Our Vision

To become a trusted bakery by delivering outstanding products, embracing innovation and building strong customer relationships.

Our Values

Quality, customer satisfaction, creativity, community and excellent service.
